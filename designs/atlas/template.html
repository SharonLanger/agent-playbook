EXPLORATION ONLY / not implemented

Reviewer: request → events → domain result

Non-binding candidate scenario. The provider changes; the role handles our event types. The workflow decides what happens after a validated review.

  1. 1 · Application validates settings and injects the selected executor into the reviewer.
  2. 2 · CrewAI workflow reaches the review step and supplies typed ReviewInput.
  3. 3 · Reviewer prepares instructions, output schema and required capabilities.
  4. 4 · Execution checks compatibility and starts one invocation.
  5. 5 · Provider adapter consumes the native stream and maps messages into our AgentEvent union.
  6. 6 · One dispatcher delivers each event to the role handler and telemetry projection.
  7. 7 · Reviewer checks the technical outcome and validates ReviewResult against collected evidence.
  8. 8 · Workflow approves, requests a bounded revision, or takes an explicit failure/inconclusive route.
Three different outcomes: a tool may fail; the SDK invocation may still finish successfully; the reviewer may return changes_required. Keep all three meanings visible.
